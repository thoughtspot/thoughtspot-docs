= Share Models
:last_updated: 12/31/2020
:linkattrs:
:experimental:
:page-partial:
:description: You can share Models with users or with groups.

NOTE: Worksheets are now referred to as Models.

You can share Models with users or with groups.

Sharing a Model allows users to select it as a data source and search it. When you share a Model, you give users or groups within your cluster access to the Model's data.
You share all of its columns.
Sharing a Model does not share the underlying tables.
If you want to share the underlying tables, see xref:share-source-tables.adoc[Sharing tables and columns].

If you want to migrate the Model to another cluster, or otherwise export it, see xref:scriptability.adoc[Migrate or restore objects].

A Model can be shared by the owner of the Model, or by an administrator.
Users can start searching a Model as soon as the Model is shared with them.

You can share a Model from the list of Models under *Models* <<share-datatab,on the *Data* tab>>, or from <<share-dataset,within the Model>>.
