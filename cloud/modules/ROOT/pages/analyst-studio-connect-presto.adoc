== Connect to Presto

. Log in to {product} and follow the steps to xref:analyst-studio-connecting-analyst-studio-to-your-database.adoc#direct-connect[connect a database to {product}].
. Click the *Search databases* dropdown and select *Presto*.
. Enter the Display name, Description (optional), Host, Port, Default Catalog (optional), and Default Schema (optional).
. Select the preferred JDBC driver.
. (Optional) Click the checkbox to use authentication. If selected, enter the LDAP Username and LDAP Password.
. Click *Connect*.
You will land on a page that displays your database schema.
