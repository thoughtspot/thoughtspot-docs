== Supported JDBC drivers and known issues

* (Experimental) Clickhouse version 0.9.8
* (Recommended) Clickhouse version 0.6.1 
* (Experimental) Clickhouse-jdbc v0.3.2-patch11

There are no known issues with this driver.

== Connect to ClickHouse

. Log in to {product} and follow the steps to xref:analyst-studio-connecting-analyst-studio-to-your-database.adoc#direct-connect[connect a database to {product}].
. Click the *Search databases* dropdown and select *Clickhouse*.
. Enter the Display name, Description (optional), Host, Port, Database name, Username, and Password.
. Select the preferred JDBC driver.
. Click *Connect*.
You will land on a page that displays your database schema.
