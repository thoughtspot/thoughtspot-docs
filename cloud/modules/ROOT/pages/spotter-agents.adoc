ThoughtSpot Spotter Agents are a suite of AI-powered agents that automate every stage of the analytics workflow — from building semantic data models and generating visualizations to writing analytics code and answering business questions in natural language.

Spotter, the conversational AI agent at the center of the platform, lets any user ask questions in plain language and get trusted, data-grounded answers instantly — including deeper analytical questions such as "Why did sales drop last quarter?" — and can connect to external tools like Slack, Confluence, and Jira to push insights and actions beyond ThoughtSpot.

Companion agents handle the setup work: SpotterModel builds and refines semantic models from a natural language description, SpotterViz assembles a complete, publication-ready Liveboard automatically, and SpotterCode generates the analytics code your team would otherwise write by hand.

Together, Spotter Agents give every member of your organization an AI collaborator at each step of the analytics journey, reducing time-to-insight and the manual effort required to deliver governed analytics at scale.

In addition to our most prominent agent, Spotter itself, we have built agents to simplify and streamline the process of digesting your data and surfacing insights.

== SpotterModel [.badge.badge-early-access]#Early Access#

You can now use SpotterModel to generate comprehensive, optimized Models ready to use in your analysis. SpotterModel automates table selection, join creation, and column selection, while allowing you to review and customize each step.

For more information, see xref:spotter-model-create.adoc[].


== SpotterViz [.badge.badge-early-access]#Early Access#

ThoughtSpot can now autonomously create Liveboards based on your prompts, populating them with relevant visualizations and KPIs. In response to your query, ThoughtSpot evaluates available data sources, creates analysis, and lays out the information for you. After creating a Liveboard, you can enter more prompts in SpotterViz at any time to revise and refine it. You can also give feedback by using the thumbs up and thumbs down buttons.

For more information, see xref:spotter-viz.adoc[].
