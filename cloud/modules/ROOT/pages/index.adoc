[.lead]
ThoughtSpot Cloud is a cloud-native, AI-powered analytics platform that puts self-service
analysis in the hands of everyone in your organization—no SQL or data expertise required.

Connect to your cloud data warehouse and search your data in plain language with
xref:spotter.adoc[Spotter], build and share interactive Liveboards, and automate analytics
workflows with AI agents—all without
moving your data.
