== Number and date formatting changes
// Rani. jira: SCAL-345830. docs jira: SCAL-350136. source: "JDK 17 Locale Format Sign-off" table in Sidharth's Slack thread.
// PM: Sejal (sign-off). Eng: Abhishek Ranjan, Sidharth Mishra.

Starting with ThoughtSpot Cloud 26.11.0.cl, ThoughtSpot uses updated international locale data. As a result, some numbers, currencies, and dates are formatted slightly differently in the following locales. Your data and ThoughtSpot features do not change.

[options="header",cols="2,2,5"]
|===
| Locale | Format | What changes

| English (United Kingdom) (en-GB)
| Dates and abbreviated month names
| The abbreviation for September changes from `Sep` to `Sept`. For example, `3 Sep 2026` becomes `3 Sept 2026`.

.2+| English (India) (en-IN)
| Currency
| The space after the rupee sign is removed. For example, `₹ 1,234,567.89` becomes `₹1,234,567.89`.

| Short date and time
| `PM` changes to lowercase `pm`. For example, `03/09/26, 2:05 PM` becomes `03/09/26, 2:05 pm`.

| Español (es-ES)
| Dates and abbreviated month names
| The period after the abbreviation for September is removed. For example, `3 sept. 2026` becomes `3 sept 2026`.

| Français (France) (fr-FR)
| Numbers and currency
| The thousands separator changes from a no-break space to a narrow no-break space. The value looks almost the same, but text comparisons and copied values can differ.

| Português (Brasil) (pt-BR)
| Dates and abbreviated month names
| A period is added after the abbreviation for September. For example, `3 de set de 2026` becomes `3 de set. de 2026`.

| 中文(简体) (zh-CN)
| Currency
| The yuan sign changes from the full-width `￥` to `¥`. For example, `￥1,234,567.89` becomes `¥1,234,567.89`.

| Nederland (nl-NL)
| Short date and time
| The year changes from two digits to four digits. For example, `03-09-26 14:05` becomes `03-09-2026 14:05`.
|===

Formatting does not change in the English (United States) (en-US), Deutsch (de-DE), Italiano (it-IT), 日本語 (ja-JP), 한국어 (ko-KR), Pусский (ru-RU), and Svenska (sv-SE) locales.
