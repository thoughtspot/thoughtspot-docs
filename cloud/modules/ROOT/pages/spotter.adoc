Spotter is your personal AI analyst, delivering business-ready insights with enterprise-grade trust. Spotter combines the most simple-to-use interface with the industry’s most rigorous approach to governance and security to AI, incorporating human intelligence at every turn.

Spotter is built on top of the BI industry’s leading relational search model, which adds business context from your underlying data model to deliver highly interpretable results with unmatched accuracy.

== Spotter experiences

There are three Spotter experiences available:

Spotter 3 [.badge.badge-early-access]#Early Access#:: Spotter 3 adds the capability to query structured and unstructured data together, gaining a better understanding of your data. Spotter 3 can plan, reason, iterate, and execute complex analysis like a human analyst. You can use Auto Mode to automatically select the appropriate data source for your queries, and switch between sources within the same conversation. Spotter 3 also introduces the capability to integrate your own tools into the conversation, such as Slack, Confluence, or Jira, allowing you to pull data from external sources and push actions to the selected tool.


Spotter Agent (Spotter 2)::
Spotter Agent is an AI analyst that delivers agentic data experiences with enterprise-grade trust and scale. Spotter Agent can suggest questions you can ask to improve your understanding of the data analysis, and you can ask Spotter to answer multiple questions at once. You can also ask questions about how calculated fields such as formulas are generated. Spotter Agent has also improved its responses for questions not related to the data model you selected.


Spotter Classic (Spotter 1)::
Our classic conversational analytics experience, which allows you to ask questions and get answers from your data in natural language. It understands your data questions, and shows answers with state of the art visualizations. Spotter is designed to empower non-technical teams to self-serve actionable insights from data and scale reliable, data-driven decision-making across the organization.
