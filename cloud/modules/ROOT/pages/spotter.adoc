== Spotter experiences

There are three Spotter experiences available:
[#spotter3]
Spotter 3:: Spotter 3 adds the capability to query structured and unstructured data together, gaining a better understanding of your data. Spotter 3 can plan, reason, iterate, and execute complex analysis like a human analyst. You can use Auto mode to automatically select the appropriate data source for your queries, and switch between sources within the same conversation. Spotter 3 also introduces the capability to integrate your own tools into the conversation, such as Slack, Confluence, or Jira, allowing you to pull data from external sources and push actions to the selected tool. If you don't find a particular feature on your ThoughtSpot instance, contact your ThoughtSpot administrator.
+
Specific Spotter 3 capabilities are identified with a [.badge.badge-spotter3]#Spotter 3# badge. For more information, see xref:spotter-getting-started.adoc[]. To see Spotter 3 in action, watch the xref:spotter3-in-action[Spotter 3 introduction].

Spotter Agent (Spotter 2)::
Spotter Agent is an AI analyst that delivers agentic data experiences with enterprise-grade trust and scale. Spotter Agent can suggest questions you can ask to improve your understanding of the data analysis, and you can ask Spotter to answer multiple questions at once. You can also ask questions about how calculated fields such as formulas are generated. Spotter Agent has also improved its responses for questions not related to the data model you selected.


Spotter Classic (Spotter 1)::
Our classic conversational analytics experience, which allows you to ask questions and get answers from your data in natural language. It understands your data questions, and shows answers with state of the art visualizations. Spotter is designed to empower non-technical teams to self-serve actionable insights from data and scale reliable, data-driven decision-making across the organization.
