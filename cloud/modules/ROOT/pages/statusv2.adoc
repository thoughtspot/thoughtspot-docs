To view real-time updates on the status of ThoughtSpot Cloud services, go to the ThoughtSpot Status page at: https://status.thoughtspot.cloud/[https://status.thoughtspot.cloud/^]. Here you can find information and updates about service interruptions in different geographical regions, as well as a list of past incidents.

== Status information

The ThoughtSpot Status page allows you to track the real-time status of important services:

* Spotter
* MCP Server
* Analyst Studio
* ThoughtSpot Sync
* Other Services

The color of the status message reflects the health of the service.

[width="100%", cols="20%,80%, options="header"]
|====================
| *Color* | *Status*
| [.green]#Green# | Operational
| [.yellow]#Yellow# | Degraded performance
| [.orange]#Orange# | Partial outage
| [.red]#Red# | Service disruption
| [.blue]#Blue# | Under maintenance
|====================

== Subscribe to updates

You can subscribe to receive status updates through email, text, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Atom or RSS feeds. To subscribe, click the *SUBSCRIBE TO UPDATES* button.

If you are experiencing problems and do not see a notice posted please contact {support-url} for assistance.
