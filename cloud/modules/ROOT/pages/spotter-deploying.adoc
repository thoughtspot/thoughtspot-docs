This section contains the information required to deploy Spotter. For best results, follow these steps in order:

. xref:spotter-before-you-start.adoc[Preparing for Spotter]
.. Identify who you're coaching Spotter for.
.. Collect real questions and group them.
.. Optimize your data model.
.. Test Spotter.
. xref:spotter-enable.adoc[]
.. Turn on AI features for your instance.
.. Enable Spotter for a user group.
.. Enable Spotter for a Liveboard.
. xref:spotter-conversation-history-enable.adoc[Enabling Chat History]
. xref:spotter-byollmk.adoc[Connecting to your LLM]
.. Learn about your options for Large Language Models.
. xref:spotter-conversations-liveboard.adoc[]
.. Get insight on how your users are interacting with Spotter.
