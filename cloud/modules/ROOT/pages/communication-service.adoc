The ThoughtSpot communication service delivers notifications to ThoughtSpot users. When a user shares an object, requests access, or receives an alert, the service sends the notification by email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or in the ThoughtSpot notification center.

The communication service is available on ThoughtSpot Cloud, for both ThoughtSpot Analytics and ThoughtSpot Embedded. It is not available on ThoughtSpot Software (on-premises) deployments.

== Benefits

When the communication service is enabled on your ThoughtSpot instance, you get the following:

Notification preferences:: Each user can choose which notifications they receive, and on which channel, from the *Notification preferences* section of their profile. This section appears only when the communication service is enabled.
Styled, localized emails:: Notification emails use a consistent, styled template, and are translated into the user's ThoughtSpot language.
Email customization:: Administrators can change the logo, colors, terminology, and footer of notification emails, for all Orgs or for individual Orgs. This option appears only when the communication service is enabled.
In-app notifications:: Users see share notifications, access requests, and alerts in the ThoughtSpot notification center, in addition to email.
Reliable delivery:: The service is highly available, and delivers notifications on multiple channels from a single source.


== Notifications sent by the communication service

The communication service sends notifications for the following events:

* A Liveboard, Answer, or visualization is shared with you
* A Spotter chat is shared with you
* A user requests access to a Liveboard or Answer that you can edit
* A KPI or Monitor alert is triggered
* SpotIQ analysis results are ready
* Account events: welcome and account activation, invitations, password reset, and locked accounts

Liveboard schedule emails can also be delivered through the communication service. To enable this for your instance, contact {support-url}.

=== Features that require the communication service

Some notifications are available only when the communication service is enabled:

* *Spotter chat share notifications.* Users receive an email and in-app notification when a Spotter chat is shared with them. Without the communication service, shared chats generate no notification, and recipients can reach a shared chat only through its link.
* *Notification preferences* in the user profile.
* *Email customizations* in *Admin settings*.
* Webhooks


== How users manage notifications

When the communication service is enabled, each user controls their own notifications:

. Select your user icon, then select *Profile*.
. Next to *Notification preferences*, select *Edit*.
. Select or clear the checkbox for each notification and channel.
. Select *Save*.

For more information, see xref:user-profile.adoc#notification-prefs[Notification preferences] and xref:web-notifications.adoc[Web notifications].

== How administrators customize emails

Administrators can change the branding, terminology, and footer content of notification emails from *Admin settings* > *Email customizations*. For more information, see xref:customize-email-template.adoc[Customize ThoughtSpot email template].

== Check whether the service is enabled

Some existing ThoughtSpot Cloud instances, including some ThoughtSpot Embedded instances, might not have the communication service enabled.

To check, select your user icon, then select *Profile*. If your profile doesn't have a *Notification preferences* section, the communication service is not enabled on your instance. To enable it, contact {support-url}.


////
== Before you enable the service

If the communication service is not enabled on your instance, review the following before you ask for it to be enabled:

* *Email appearance.* Notification emails change from a plain-text style to the styled template. Users see the ThoughtSpot logo and terminology until an administrator rebrands the emails with xref:customize-email-template.adoc[email customization], including the logo, colors, terminology, and footer.
* *Custom sender address.* If your instance sends notification emails from a custom sender address, ThoughtSpot Support confirms how this address applies to emails sent by the communication service before enabling it.
* *Notification defaults.* Email and in-app notifications for shared objects and access requests are on by default. Users who don't want these notifications can turn them off in their profile.
* *Test first.* If you have a development or staging instance, enable the service there first and review the emails your users will receive.

[#enable-service]
== Enable the communication service

To enable the communication service on your instance, contact {support-url}. Include your ThoughtSpot instance URL in your request.

The change does not require downtime. After the service is enabled:

* The *Notification preferences* section appears in each user's profile.
* *Email customizations* appears under *Application settings* in *Admin settings*.
* New notifications use the communication service. Notifications sent before the change are not resent.
