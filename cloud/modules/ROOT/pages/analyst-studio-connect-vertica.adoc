== Connect to Vertica

. Log in to {product} and follow the steps to xref:analyst-studio-connecting-analyst-studio-to-your-database.adoc#bridge[connect a database to {product}].
. Click the *Search databases* dropdown and select *Vertica*.
. Choose your operating system:
+
--
* OSX
* Ubuntu
* CentOS
* Debian
* Redhat
* Windows
--
. Download the file to install the Bridge connector, and follow the terminal instructions.
. Click *Connect*.
You will land on a page that displays your database schema.
