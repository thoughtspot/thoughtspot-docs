Administrators can review any Spotter chat in their Org without leaving Spotter, from the *All chats* list.

[NOTE]
====
Spotter monitoring is available in the Liveboard side panel (New) Spotter 3
experience. In the Answer overlay (Classic) experience and in Spotter 2, use the
xref:spotter-conversations-liveboard.adoc[Spotter Conversations Liveboard].
====

== Prerequisites

* You must have administrator privileges. Users without administrator privileges don't see the monitoring options.
* In a multi-Org instance, monitoring shows data for the Org you are signed in to. To see another Org, switch to that Org.

== Open Spotter monitoring

. Open Spotter.
. Click *Settings* at the bottom of the left panel.
. Select *Monitor all chats* to open the *All chats* list.

== Review all chats

*All chats* lists every Spotter chat in your Org, with the most recently modified first, snf 20 per page. The list shows the *Chat* name, *Analyst*, *Model*, *Author Name*, and *Last modified* time of each chat.

To find a chat:

. In *Search*, enter part of the chat name.
. To narrow the list, click *Add filter* and select *Last modified*, *Analyst*, *Model*, *Author*, *Origin*, or *Conversation ID*.

To read a chat:

. Click on its name.
+
The chat opens in read-only view, with each question, the time it was asked, and Spotter's answer as the user saw it.
. Click *Show work* to see how Spotter reached the answer.
. Click *Close* to return to the list.
+
Your search and filters remain applied.

== Monitoring when chat history is turned off

*All chats* lists only chats that are saved as chat history. If chat history is turned off, new chats are not listed in *All chats*, and saved chats are hidden immediately, and permanently deleted 15 days later. For more information, see
xref:spotter-data-handling.adoc[Spotter 3 data handling].
