Administrators can review any Spotter chat in their Org without leaving Spotter, from the *All chats* list. Administrators can also track how Spotter is used and how well it answers questions, from the Spotter metrics Liveboard.

[NOTE]
====
Spotter monitoring is available in the Liveboard side panel (New) Spotter 3
experience. In the Answer overlay (Classic) experience and in Spotter 2, use the
xref:spotter-conversations-liveboard.adoc[Spotter Conversations Liveboard].
====

== Prerequisites

* You must have administrator privileges. Users without administrator privileges don't see the monitoring options.
* In a multi-Org instance, monitoring shows data for the Org you are signed in to. To see another Org, switch to that Org.

. Open Spotter.
. Click *Settings* at the bottom of the left panel.
. Select one of the following options:
* *Monitor all chats* to open the *All chats* list.
* *Monitor metrics* to open the Spotter metrics Liveboard.

== Review all chats

*All chats* lists every Spotter chat in your Org, with the most recently modified first, and 20 per page. The list shows the *Chat* name, *Analyst*, *Model*, *Author Name*, and *Last modified* time of each chat.

To find a chat:

. In *Search*, enter part of the chat name.
. To narrow the list, click *Add filter* and select *Last modified*, *Analyst*, *Model*, *Author*, *Origin*, or *Conversation ID*.

To read a chat:

. Click its name.
+
The chat opens in read-only view, with each question, the time it was asked, and Spotter's answer as the user saw it.
. Click *Show work* to see how Spotter reached the answer.
. Click *Close* to return to the list.
+
Your search and filters remain applied.

== Monitor Spotter metrics

The Spotter metrics Liveboard shows aggregate data about how Spotter is used in your Org and how well it answers questions. Use it to track adoption trends, find conversations that need attention, and monitor answer quality over time.

To enable Spotter metrics, contact ThoughtSpot Support.

The Liveboard includes the following information:

Usage:: How many users and chats use Spotter over time, and which data models and Spotter Analysts they use.

Feedback:: Conversations with feedback, downvoted conversations, the split of upvotes and downvotes, and the users who give the most feedback. You can view feedback by data model and by Spotter Analyst.

Performance:: How long Spotter takes to respond, including average and 90th percentile (p90) end-to-end response time, the distribution of response times, and errors by type.

NOTE: Metrics are refreshed periodically. New Spotter activity can take up to two hours to appear on the Liveboard.

== Monitoring when chat history is turned off

*All chats* lists only chats that are saved as chat history. If chat history is turned off, new chats are not listed in *All chats*, and saved chats are hidden immediately, and permanently deleted 15 days later. For more information, see
xref:spotter-data-handling.adoc[Spotter 3 data handling].
