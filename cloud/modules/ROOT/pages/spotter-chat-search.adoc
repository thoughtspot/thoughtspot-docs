Search your saved Spotter chats to find a past analysis and continue it. Spotter matches your search against the chat title, the questions you asked, and Spotter's answers.

NOTE: Search is available in the Answer overlay (Classic) Spotter 3 experience. It is not available in the Liveboard side panel (New) experience yet.

== Before you begin

* Chat history must be turned on for your instance. See xref:spotter-conversation-history-enable.adoc[].

* Only saved chats are searchable. Chats you delete, and chats removed by the retention period, no longer appear in results.

== Search your chats

To search your chats, follow these steps:

. Open Spotter.
. In the Spotter sidebar, click Search chats (the search icon next to Chats). The Chats page opens and lists your chats, newest first.
+
[.bordered]
image::search-chats.png[]
. Type in the Search box. Results update as you type, and more load as you scroll.
+
[.bordered]
image::chats-searched.png[]
. Click a chat to open it at its latest message. You can ask follow-up questions as usual.
. If your search matches no existing chats, the Search chats interface shows *No chat history matched your search*.
