Search your saved Spotter chats to find a past analysis and continue it. Spotter matches your search against the chat title, the questions you asked, and Spotter's answers.

// SOURCE: SCAL-348754
NOTE: Search is available in Spotter 3. For the Spotter versions, see xref:spotter-versions.adoc[Spotter versions].

== Before you begin

* Chat history must be turned on for your instance. See xref:spotter-conversation-history-enable.adoc[].

* Only saved chats are searchable. Chats you delete, and chats removed by the retention period, no longer appear in results.

== Search your chats

To search your chats, follow these steps:

. Open Spotter.
. In the Spotter sidebar, click Search chats (the search icon next to Chats). The Chats page opens and lists your chats, newest first.
+
[.bordered]
image::search-chats.png[]
. Type in the Search box. Results update as you type, and more load as you scroll.
+
[.bordered]
image::chats-searched.png[]
. Click a chat to open it at its latest message. You can ask follow-up questions as usual.
. If your search matches no existing chats, the Search chats interface shows *No chat history matched your search*.

== Search your chats in Spotter on Liveboards

// TODO: verify with engineering — EA or GA in 26.12.0.cl. SRE KT (Confluence OS/5254840321) says EA, SRE-enabled per tenant. If EA, add "To enable this feature, contact ThoughtSpot Support."
In xref:spotter-liveboard-experience.adoc[Spotter on Liveboards], you can search across your saved Spotter conversations directly from the chat panel, without scrolling through the full history list.

A *Search* box appears at the top of the chat panel. As you type, Spotter searches both conversation titles and full transcripts, and returns matching conversations with the match highlighted.


[cols="2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Feature|Description
|Typo-tolerant matching|Results tolerate minor spelling errors. For example, a search for `forcast` finds `forecast`.

|Your conversations only|Results include only your own saved chats. Conversations shared with you are not included.

|Results within 5 seconds|New and renamed conversations become searchable within 5 seconds of the change.
// TODO: verify with engineering — SCAL-333707 lists near-real-time indexing as out of scope. Confirm the 5-second figure.

|Filters|Narrow results by date range, data model, or agent.

|Saved chats only|The search scope matches your chat history list exactly. Unsaved chats cannot be searched. If chat history is turned off, the *Search* box is not available.
|===
Conversation search is available in Spotter 3 on ThoughtSpot Cloud.
