Spotter 3 focuses on linear continuity. You can append new questions to any past thread to continue where you left off.

NOTE: Advanced outputs such as *forecasts*, Python-based analysis, or temporary data artifacts are never stored. When you resume a conversation, Spotter 3 automatically regenerates these artifacts to ensure they reflect the most current data available. This may lead to a data drift where your analysis and fresh data might be out of sync.

== Organizing Your History

The History Rail on the left-hand side of the Spotter interface serves as your primary navigation hub.

* Auto-Naming: Spotter uses your first question (for example, "What was the sales trend for 2024?") to automatically title the chat.
* Time-Based Sorting: The most recently updated conversations appear at the top of the list.
* Custom Renaming and Highlighting: To make important threads easier to find, you can rename your chats.

TIP: Use emojis in your titles (for example, 🚀 Executive Review or 📈 Q3 Revenue) to visually highlight specific conversations for quick back-reference.
