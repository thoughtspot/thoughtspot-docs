This guide provides the essential steps for an administrator to set up AgentSpot, including managing users, and configuring connectors to ensure your team is ready to use AgentSpot.

The interface is divided into three primary sections:

* **Setup:** Core account configurations
* **Governance:** Management of users, groups, and permissions
* **Monitoring:** High-level usage statistics and conversation tracking

== Managing users
To add users to AgentSpot, you must first add them in your ThoughtSpot instance. To do that, you create a role with AgentSpot privileges, assign that role to a group, and then add users to the group, if needed.

To manage users in AgentSpot, you must do the following:

* Create a role with AgentSpot privileges.
* Add your role to a group.
* Add users to your group, as needed.

There are two primary AgentSpot privileges you can assign to a role:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Privilege | Description

| *Can manage AgentSpot* | Full administrative access to manage connectors, agents, and organization-wide settings.
| *Can use AgentSpot* | Access to AgentSpot to use shared agents.
|===

IMPORTANT: Users only appear in the AgentSpot user list once they have been assigned a role containing one of these privileges.

=== Accessing your ThoughtSpot instance

Access your ThoughtSpot instance from AgentSpot by doing the following:

. Sign in to AgentSpot.
. Click your user profile icon in the upper right, and select *Launch ThoughtSpot*.
. In ThoughtSpot, go to the *Admin Settings* tab.

=== Creating a role with AgentSpot privileges

To create a role with AgentSpot privileges, do the following:

. Under the *Admin Settings* tab, click *Roles*.
. Click *Create role*.
. In the _Create role_ window, enter a Role name and description.
. Click *AgentSpot Control*.
. Select one or more AgentSpot privileges for your role.
. Click *Review selection*.
. Review the role name, description, and admin controls.
. When you are ready to save your role, click *Save*.
. Create additional roles as needed.

=== Adding your role to a group

You can add your role to an existing group or a new group.

==== To add your role to an existing group, do the following:

. Under the Admin Settings tab, click *Groups*.
. Click the More menu associated with the group and select *Edit*.
. In the _Roles_ section, select the checkbox for the role(s) you want to add and click *Review selection*.
. Review the roles you added.
. When you are ready to save your group, click *Save*.

==== To add your role to new group, do the following:

. Click *Create group*.
. In the _Create group_ window, do the following:
.. Enter a display name, group name, and description.
.. Specify if you want to allow others to share objects with the group.
.. Under _Select roles_, choose the AgentSpot privileges you want to add to the group.
.. Click *Review selection*.
.. Review the Roles you added.
.. When you are ready to save your group, click *Save*.

=== Managing connectors
Connectors allow AgentSpot to interact with external applications like Jira, Slack, and Clay.

==== Available connectors
AgentSpot ships with native connectors for popular applications. As an administrator, you have the authority to:

* **Activate/Deactivate:** Enable or disable specific apps for the entire organization (e.g., turning off Slack write-access if desired).
* **Test & Share:** You can activate a connector for yourself to test its functionality before "publishing" or sharing it with the rest of the organization.

==== Custom connectors
If a required application is not the native list of available connectors, you can add a custom connector using **MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers**. Simply provide the URL for the MCP server and complete the setup to enable any compatible tool.

To create a custom connector in AgentSpot, do the following:

. Log in to AgentSpot.
. Click the gear icon in the upper right, to open the _Admin Settings_ page.
. Under the _Connectors_ tab, click *+Custom Connector*.
. In the _Custom Connector_ window, do the following:
.. Enter a *Connector Name*.
.. Enter a *Connector URL*.
.. (Optional) For *Transport Type*, select either of the following:
... Streamable HTTP (Recommended)
... Server-Sent Events (SSE)
.. (Optional) Enter a *Description*.
.. (Optional) Enter a *Logo URL*.
.. (Optional) Choose an *Authentication* method.
.. Click *Add Connector*.

==== User authentication and security
Connectors do not require any setup for authentication because they follow the form of authentication that the user already uses to connect with the application.

When an admin enables a connector (e.g., Jira), each user must log in with their individual credentials. AgentSpot respects all existing permissions within the target app; users will only see data and perform actions they are already authorized to access.

== Monitoring usage
The _Usage_ page provides high-level analytics to help you track the ROI and adoption of AgentSpot.

You can monitor the following:

* Total active users
* Number of conversations
* Connector usage frequency

== Monitoring users
The _Users_ page allows you to view detailed information about users and groups who use AgentSpot.

It provides the following information:

* *Users*
** *Role*: Member or Admin
** *Level*: Basic, Standard, or Power
** *Sessions*: Number of total sessions
** *Messages*: Total number of messages
** *Agents*: Total number of agents
** *Memories*: Total number of memories
** *Last Active*: Last time user was active
* *Groups*

== Agent and workflow governance
Administrators have visibility over all agents and workflows created within the organization.

* **Visibility:** Admins can see every agent created, regardless of who the owner is.
* **Business continuity:** If a user leaves the company, an administrator can step in to manage, edit, or reassign the agents and workflows they created.

== Onboarding users to specific agents
Once agents are created, you must share them with the appropriate teams to make them visible in their dashboard.

1. **Select the agent:** (e.g., "Lead Generator" or "HR Onboarding").
2. **Assign Access:** Select the specific group (e.g., "Sales Team" or "HR Group").
3. **Set Permission Level:**
** *Use:* User can interact with the agent but cannot change its configuration.
** *Edit:* User can modify the agent's logic or settings.
4. **Save & Share:** The agent will immediately appear in the user's AgentSpot library.
