:last_updated: June 10, 2026
:linkattrs:

[.lead]
Build, test, and publish your first AgentSpot agent end-to-end.

== Overview

This guide is the full builder walkthrough. Deeper topics — writing instructions, managing sessions, file uploads, sharing and versioning — have their own articles linked at each step.

By the end you'll have a published, tested agent your team can use.

== Prerequisites

* You hold the *Agent Builder* role. If the *Agents* page is read-only, ask your admin.
* Your admin has shared at least one data source and one model provider with your group.
* You can answer in one sentence: "What job will this agent do, for whom?"
* (Recommended) You have 3–5 example questions a real user would ask.

== Step 1: Plan the agent

Write these down before opening the builder. Vague agents do not get used.

* *Name* — short and recognizable (e.g. `EMEA Pipeline Assistant`).
* *Audience* — which team or group.
* *Job* — one sentence.
* *Out of scope* — what it explicitly will not do.
* *Data it needs* — specific sources, tables, or worksheets.
* *Skills it needs* — usually none for a first agent.

== Step 2: Create a new agent

. Open *Agents* > *Create agent*. ⚠️ Confirm label with [feature owner].
. Enter *Name* and a one-sentence *Short description*.
. Choose *Start from scratch* or pick a template. ⚠️ Confirm template availability.
. Select *Create*.

*Expected result:* The agent editor opens with empty *Instructions*, *Data*, *Skills*, and *Settings* tabs. ⚠️ Confirm tab names.

== Step 3: Write the instructions

This is the single most important step. Aim for under 300 words.

. Open the *Instructions* tab.
. Use the structure in xref:03-instruction-framework.adoc[Instruction framework]: *Role*, *What to do*, *What not to do*, *Tone and format*, *When unsure*.
. Save.

A worked example is in xref:03-instruction-framework.adoc#example[Instruction framework — Example].

== Step 4: Connect data

The agent can only reason over data you explicitly attach.

. Open the *Data* tab > *Add data source*.
. Pick a source your admin has shared with your group.
. Scope to specific tables, views, worksheets, or files. Attach the smallest amount of data that lets the agent do its job.
. (Strongly recommended) Add a one-line *Data description* per table — what it contains and what its key columns mean. This dramatically improves answer quality.
. Save.

For file-based agents, see xref:05-uploads-and-attachments.adoc[Uploads and attachments].

*Expected result:* Each attached source shows status *Ready*.

== Step 5: (Optional) Add skills

Skills let the agent act, not just answer.

. Open the *Skills* tab > *Add skill*. ⚠️ Confirm tab and label.
. Pick from the skill catalog your admin has enabled for your group.
. Configure the skill (e.g. default Slack channel, default Jira project).
. In *Instructions*, add a sentence telling the agent when to use each skill.

See xref:../06-skills/01-overview.adoc[Skills overview].

Skip this for your first agent unless a skill is essential.

== Step 6: Choose model and settings

. Open the *Settings* tab.
. *Model* — keep the default unless you have a reason to change it.
. *Temperature* — keep the default (low) for factual agents. ⚠️ Confirm default.
. *Response length* — set a sensible max so answers stay short.
. *Citations* — turn on. Strongly recommended.
. Save.

== Step 7: Test in the preview panel

Test before anyone else touches it.

. Open the preview panel.
. Run each of your 3–5 example questions.
. For each answer, check it's correct, cited, in scope, and matches your tone instructions.
. Run at least one adversarial question (out-of-scope or ambiguous). Confirm the agent refuses or asks for clarification rather than guessing.
. Iterate on instructions and data until reliable.

For multi-turn behavior, see xref:04-sessions-and-conversations.adoc[Sessions and conversations].

== Step 8: Publish

See xref:06-sharing-and-publishing.adoc[Sharing and publishing] for the full details. The short version:

. Select *Publish*.
. Choose audience: *Private*, *Group*, or *Organization*.
. Add release notes.
. Publish.

*Expected result:* The agent appears in the consumer agent list for the chosen audience, and a new version is recorded.

== Troubleshooting

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Symptom |What to try

|"I don't have access to that data."
|Confirm the data source is attached and that your admin shared it with your group.

|Confident but wrong answers.
|Tighten instructions ("only answer from attached data"), lower temperature, add per-table data descriptions.

|Formatting instructions are ignored.
|Move formatting to the top of the prompt; shorten the overall instructions.

|Agent answers out-of-scope questions.
|Add an explicit *What not to do* section naming the topics and re-test.

|Citations missing.
|Confirm *Citations* is on in *Settings* and that the source supports citations. ⚠️ Confirm per-source citation support.
|===
