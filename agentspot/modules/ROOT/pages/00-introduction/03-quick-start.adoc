[.lead]
Get oriented in AgentSpot in under 30 minutes. Pick the path that matches what you want to do.

== Sign in

. Open AgentSpot at your organization's URL (typically `https://your-org.thoughtspot.cloud/agentspot`). ⚠️ Confirm URL pattern with [feature owner].
. Sign in with your normal corporate credentials.
. The left navigation shows *Agents* and *Workflows*. If you're an admin, you also see *Admin*.

If you can't sign in or don't see any agents, contact your AgentSpot admin.

== Path A — I want to use an agent (5 minutes)

. Open *Agents* in the left navigation.
. Browse or search the list. Each card shows the agent's name and a one-line description.
. Open an agent to see its detail page. Read *What it can do* and *What it can't do* before asking anything.
. Select *Open chat* (⚠️ confirm label) and ask your question.
. Use thumbs-up or thumbs-down to give the agent's owner feedback.

Deeper guide: xref:../consumer/use-an-agent.adoc[Use an agent].

== Path B — I want to build an agent (30 minutes)

You need the *Agent Builder* role. If the *Create agent* button is greyed out, contact your admin.

. Open *Agents* and select *Create agent*.
. Name it and write one sentence in *Short description*.
. In *Instructions*, write a short prompt: role, what to do, what not to do, tone, what to do when unsure. Keep it under 300 words.
. In *Data*, attach one data source. Resist attaching more until you've proven the agent works on one.
. In *Settings*, leave defaults except *Citations* — turn it on.
. Use the preview panel to ask 3–5 real questions. Iterate on instructions until the answers are correct, cited, and in scope.
. Publish to a small group with release notes.

Deeper guide: xref:../01-agents/02-create-an-agent.adoc[Create an agent].

== Path C — I want to deploy AgentSpot for my organization (1–2 hours initial setup)

You need *AgentSpot Admin*. If you don't see the *Admin* section in the left navigation, contact ThoughtSpot.

. *Provision* — confirm your entitlement, turn the feature on, add a co-admin and a small pilot group.
. *Configure identity* — connect SSO so users sign in with their corporate accounts, and map IdP groups to AgentSpot roles.
. *Configure data and models* — connect at least one data source and one model provider, share each with the right group.
. *Set governance* — enable guardrails, set quotas, turn on audit logging.
. *Verify* — sign in as a builder (incognito) and run an end-to-end test agent.
