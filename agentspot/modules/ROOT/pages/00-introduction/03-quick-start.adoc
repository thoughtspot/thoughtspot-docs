Get oriented in AgentSpot in under 30 minutes. Pick the path that matches what you want to do.

== Sign in

[IMPORTANT]
====
To sign in to AgentSpot your AgentSpot admin must assign the following roles to your user:

* Can use AgentSpot
* Can manage AgentSpot
====

. Open AgentSpot by going to the *Insights* tab for your Org in your ThoughtSpot instance, and clicking *AgentSpot* in the left-hand navigation (just below _Home_ and _Spotter_).
. Sign in with your normal corporate credentials.
. The left navigation shows *Agents* and *Workflows*. If you're an admin, you also see *Admin*.

If you can't sign in or don't see any agents, contact your AgentSpot admin.

== Path A — I want to use an agent (5 minutes)

. On the AgentSpot home page, click *Browse existing agents*.
. Browse or search the list. Each card shows the agent's name with a short description of what it does.
. Click the agent top open it and start asking questions.
+
TIP: You can ask the agent itself what it can and cannot do to better understand how to get the most value out of it.

Deeper guide: xref:../consumer/use-an-agent.adoc[Use an agent].

== Path B — I want to build an agent (30 minutes)

. On the AgentSpot home page, click *Create new agent*.
. Use the Agent builder chat to describe what you want the agent to help you achieve. Through conversation, the agent builder will create the proper definition for you agent.
. Alternatively, you can edit sub-sections of the Agent definition on the right panel (instructions, connectors, etc.) manually as well.
+
TIP: To iteratively improve your agent, we recommend saving the agent, having conversations to understand its behavior, and going back to “Edit Agent” to provide the builder with feedback on what you wish the agent to do better. Then Save, try again, and keep honing your agent!

Deeper guide: xref:../01-agents/02-create-an-agent.adoc[Create an agent].

== Path C — I want to deploy AgentSpot for my organization (1–2 hours initial setup)

To deploy AgentSpot, you must be an admin for your ThoughtSpot Org. If you don't see the *Admin Settings* button next to your user profile upper right corner contact {support-url}.

. *Provision* — confirm your entitlement, turn the feature on, add a co-admin and a small pilot group.
. *Configure identity* — set up SSO in your ThoughtSpot instance, so users can sign in with their corporate accounts, and map IdP groups to AgentSpot roles. For more information, see https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/authentication[Authentication^].
. *Configure connectors and data* — we recommend connecting to the most popular apps in your organization as well as to ThoughtSpot data models to get access to facts via structured data
. *Verify* — Create an agent and test its behavior. Agents can be shared with all users - so consider sharing a useful agent for your organization to get your users started!
