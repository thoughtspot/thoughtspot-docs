{seekwell} allows you to sync your data warehouse with external apps and automate your customer data workflows.

.image:seekwell_icon.png[width=35] SeekWell End of Life (EOL) announcement
****
SeekWell will reach its End of Life (EOL) on July 31, 2026.

What does this mean for you?

[cols=".27%,73%", grid=none, frame=none]
|====================
a|*Continued Service*| Previously, in the new Spotter 3 prompt bar where custom placeholder text configured through `SpotterEmbed` did not display correctly. This is now fixed, and string overrides are applied as expected, restoring parity with the older prompt interface.
a|*Customer Data*| After the EOL date, SeekWell will no longer be accessible and ThoughtSpot will delete all remaining Customer Data within 30 days following shutdown.
a|*Service Termination*| On *July 31, 2026*, SeekWell will be shut down. Scheduled queries will no longer run, automated alerts will cease, and you will no longer be able to access the product.
|====================

*Next Steps: Transitioning to ThoughtSpot Sync*

To ensure a smooth transition and prevent any disruption to your business operations, we strongly recommend migrating your workflows well in advance of the EOL date.

To continue closing the loop from data to insight to action, we recommend transitioning your workflows to https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/thoughtspot-sync[ThoughtSpot Sync].

ThoughtSpot Sync allows you to automate your data workflows. Like SeekWell, ThoughtSpot Sync connects directly to the business apps your teams already use every day—such as Google Sheets, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, and more—ensuring your marketing, sales, and support teams always have fresh data.

*We are here to help*

We understand that migrating data tools requires time and effort, and our team is ready to help you migrate your workflows to ThoughtSpot Sync.

If you would like to see a demo of ThoughtSpot Sync, need assistance, or have questions regarding your account, please email us at seekwell@thoughtspot.com.

Thank you for being a valued member of the SeekWell community. We look forward to continuing to support your data journey with ThoughtSpot Sync.
****
